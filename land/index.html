The Practice

Three disciplines, one craft.

Custos works the way a law firm or an architectural studio works. We sit with leadership, we draw what should be built, and we leave the institution with a craft of its own.

I.

Consult

We sit with your leadership to map the work that matters, surface the AI that fits, and design what stays in your control. Every engagement starts with listening for a season before a line of code is written.

II.

Build

We deliver agents and tools that live inside your Microsoft 365 tenant, alongside a local Jarvis your people own outright. Everything is configured against your data, your policies, and your people.

III.

Train

We hand off a prompt library and the habits to use it, so the work compounds long after we leave. Your faculty, your staff, and your stewards keep the craft after the consultancy goes home.

Architecture

Two layers. Both yours.

Custos builds in two places, and the institution owns every part of both.

  • I.

    Agents in your tenant

    Copilots, workflows, and assistants live inside the Microsoft 365 tenant your institution already pays for. No new vendor, no shared model, no data sitting on a foreign server.

  • II.

    Jarvis on the laptop

    A local assistant for the people who carry the most weight. It runs on their machine, holds their context, and never asks for a subscription to keep working.

  • III.

    Prompts as the bridge

    The connective tissue is a library of prompts your team can read, edit, and outlast us with. Configurations rot. A craft compounds.

The work follows from the name. Protect what is sacred to the institutions that built their people, and leave the people stronger than you found them. AI belongs inside the walls of the ones who answer to its outcomes.
